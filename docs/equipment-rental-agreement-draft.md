# LiDAR.Rentals Equipment Rental Agreement — Draft for Legal Review

**Draft only — not approved for use.** This template is intended for equipment rentals in Ontario, Canada. Have an Ontario lawyer review and adapt it before customers are asked to accept it. Complete the commercial details (including fees, insurance requirements, limits, and return address) before launch.

## 1. Parties and rental record

This Equipment Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is between **LiDAR.Rentals** ("Lessor") and the customer identified in the rental checkout ("Renter"). The checkout order, equipment list, quantities, rental dates, delivery/collection details, and price form part of this Agreement.

For a business renter, the person accepting confirms they are authorized to bind that business. The person accepting must be at least 18 years old and legally capable of entering into a contract.

## 2. Rental period, delivery, and return

The rental period is the start and end date shown in the order confirmation. The Renter must make the equipment available for return by the agreed return date and follow the provided packing, shipping, collection, and handling instructions. The Renter must contact LiDAR.Rentals promptly if delivery is delayed, equipment is missing, or a return may be late. A rental extension is effective only when confirmed in writing by LiDAR.Rentals; additional charges may apply.

## 3. Permitted use and care

The Renter will use the equipment only for lawful purposes, in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and any safety guidance supplied with it. The Renter will ensure that operators are trained and competent, protect the equipment from weather and environmental hazards beyond its rated specifications, and not modify, open, repair, lend, sub-rent, or transfer possession of it without written permission. The Renter must stop using equipment that appears unsafe or defective and notify LiDAR.Rentals promptly.

## 4. Inspection, loss, damage, and theft

The Renter should inspect the equipment promptly on receipt and report visible damage or missing components without delay. While the equipment is in the Renter's care, the Renter is responsible, to the extent permitted by law and subject to any agreed insurance or protection plan, for loss, theft, failure to return, and damage beyond ordinary wear and tear. The Renter must promptly report theft or suspected criminal activity to the appropriate authorities and provide the report/reference number to LiDAR.Rentals. No repair or replacement charge is set by this draft; any applicable schedule of charges must be disclosed to the Renter before acceptance.

## 5. Fees, payment, and taxes

The checkout displays the rental price and any delivery, return, optional accessory, tax, deposit, or other charge applicable to the order. The Renter authorizes payment of the displayed amount through the payment provider. Additional charges must have a contractual basis and be disclosed as required by law. A payment receipt does not change the rental dates or other terms of this Agreement.

## 6. Late returns and extensions

If equipment is not made available for return on time, the Renter must contact LiDAR.Rentals immediately. Any late fee, extension rate, recovery cost, or consequential charge must be clearly disclosed in the order or a separate fee schedule before acceptance and must be lawful and reasonable. This draft does not itself establish a late-fee amount.

## 7. Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation, rescheduling, deposits, and refunds are governed by the policy presented to the Renter before payment, subject to applicable consumer-protection law. If no policy is presented, this draft does not invent one; the parties should resolve the matter in accordance with applicable law.

## 8. Risk, liability, and indemnity

The Renter is responsible for choosing equipment suitable for the intended task and for following applicable laws, site rules, and safety requirements. Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, including rights or remedies under applicable Ontario consumer-protection law. Any limitation of liability, waiver, or indemnity must be specifically reviewed by Ontario counsel before use. The Renter does not waive rights that cannot legally be waived.

## 9. Data and electronic acceptance

The Renter agrees that the order details and electronic acceptance record may be retained to document the transaction, subject to LiDAR.Rentals' privacy practices and applicable law. The electronic record should identify the agreement version, order reference, acceptance timestamp, and the signer’s typed name or signature capture. A copy should be made available to the Renter. Signature images and signed agreement files should be stored in private storage and not exposed through public URLs.

## 10. General terms

Any change to this Agreement must be confirmed in writing by both parties. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply to the extent permitted by law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario and the applicable laws of Canada, without overriding mandatory legal protections.

## Acceptance

By checking the acceptance box and signing electronically, the Renter confirms that they have read and understood this Agreement, had an opportunity to ask questions, and agree to its terms for the equipment listed in the associated order.

**Do not publish this draft until Ontario counsel has reviewed it and all order-specific fees and policies are clearly disclosed.**
